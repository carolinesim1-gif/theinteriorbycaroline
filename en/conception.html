— final year project —

in the woods —
a living clearing

graduation project in interior architecture at LISAA — designing a family home in Verrières-le-Buisson for the Vassili-Sevada family. an architecture rooted in nature, built in wood, open to light, structured around a central living-clearing at the heart of the plan.

wood, raw materials, verticality — an architectural language that translates both domestic warmth and connection to the living world.

single family home · verrières-le-buisson
